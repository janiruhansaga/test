Meet the Artist

Oshadha & Broody

Designs by OVKB was born out of a desire to bring back the warmth of physical cards in a digital age. Founded by Oshadha, the brand focuses on the intersection of modern minimalism and emotional depth.

Based in his home studio, Oshadha finds inspiration in the subtle details of nature and architecture. Whether it's a birthday, a wedding, or a simple "thinking of you," each card is designed to be a piece of art that stays long after the occasion has passed.

The workspace is rarely quiet, though. Oshadha's constant companion, Broody—a spirited and loving golden-hearted dog—is always close by, often sleeping under the desk as the designs come to life.

Together, they ensure that every card leaving the studio carries a bit of that comfort and joy to its destination.
